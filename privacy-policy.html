Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Introduction

HFB ("we", "us", "our") makes casual mobile games for Android (the "Games"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the Games collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It applies to every game we publish, and to this website. By using a Game you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Game.

We aim to follow applicable data protection laws, including the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and Google Play's developer policies.

2. Information we collect

You do not need to create an account to play our Games, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contact list. The Games have no chat or user-generated content.

2.1 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, app version, and crash and performance data.
  • Identifiers: the Android advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in your device settings) and an app instance ID generated by our analytics provider.
  • Approximate location: derived from your IP address (country or region level). We do not access precise GPS location. Your IP address is processed by our service providers to deliver ads, detect fraud and keep the Games secure.
  • Gameplay and usage data: levels played, progress, session length, in-game actions, feature use, and your interactions with ads.
  • Purchase information: whether a purchase was completed, and the product purchased. Payments are processed by Google Play. We never see or store your card or bank details.

2.2 Information stored on your device

Your progress, settings and in-game items are saved locally on your device. We do not receive this save data. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage deletes it.

2.3 Permissions

The Games request only the permissions needed to run, such as internet access and network state, which are used for ads, analytics and purchases. We do not request camera, microphone, contacts or precise location permissions.

3. How we use information

  • To run, maintain and fix the Games, and to keep them secure.
  • To understand how players use the Games (for example where players stop playing) so we can improve levels, balance and design.
  • To show ads, including optional rewarded ads, and to measure how ads perform.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To respond to support requests, and to comply with legal obligations.

Where the law requires your consent (for example, for personalised ads or analytics in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for it when you first open a Game, through a consent form, and you can change your choice later in the Game's settings, where available.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed in section 4, to the extent needed for them to provide their services;
  • to comply with the law, a court order or a lawful request from authorities;
  • to protect our rights, users or the public, and to prevent fraud or abuse;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will tell you if your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy; and
  • in aggregated or anonymised form that cannot identify you.

6. Your choices

  • Ad personalisation: on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads to opt out of ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID.
  • Consent: where the Game shows a consent form, you can review or withdraw your choice in the Game's settings.
  • Rewarded ads are optional. You can play without watching any ad. Supporter or remove-ads purchases, where offered, turn off ad prompts.
  • Reset or delete local data: uninstall the Game or clear its storage in Android settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent, to receive a copy of your data, and to complain to your data protection authority. California residents also have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

Most of the data described above is held by our service providers against a device identifier rather than your name, so to help us find it please include your advertising ID or app instance ID in your request. Email us at the address in section 12 and we will respond within the time required by law.

8. Data retention

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purposes in this policy. Analytics data is kept for a limited period configured in the analytics tool (no longer than 14 months) and is then deleted automatically. Information held by ad providers is retained under their own policies.

9. Children

Our Games are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by local law, such as 16 in some EEA countries). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security and international transfers

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of a Game after a change means you accept the updated policy.
